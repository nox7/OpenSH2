- +4 bytes = Frequency setting
	- 0 = Off
	- 1 = Very low
	- 2 = Low
	- 3 = Normal
	- 4 = High
	- 5 = Very high
- +4 bytes = Maximum outlaws
- +4 bytes = Location
	- 0 = All map
	- 1 = Own estate (Where the camp is placed)
	- 2 = neighboring estates
	- 3 = Own and neighboring estates
	- 4 = Human estate
